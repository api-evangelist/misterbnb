"LGBTQ+ traveler" is not a demographic - it is six of them at minimum, each with fundamentally different destination choices, accommodation preferences, booking behaviors, and on-the-ground priorities. The homogenization of LGBTQ+ travel into a single marketing category is one of the most persistent errors in travel industry thinking. This report uses misterb&b platform data to identify 6 distinct LGBTQ+ traveler profiles - not by identity label, but by actual travel behavior: what they search for, what they book, where they go, and what they need from a host.
