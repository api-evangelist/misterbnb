Ask most gay travelers about Spain's LGBTQ+ history, and they'll mention Sitges, or Madrid's Chueca , or Barcelona's Eixample. Almost nobody mentions Costa Brava — and that's a shame, because one stretch of this coastline has a queer history just as real as any of them, built by one man, one bar at a time, under a dictatorship that made it illegal.
