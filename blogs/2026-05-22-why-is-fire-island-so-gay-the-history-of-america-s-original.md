Ask most people why Fire Island is gay, and they'll shrug and say it just is — a beach, a ferry, a reputation. But Fire Island's queer identity isn't a marketing story or a recent trend. It's close to a century old, tied to specific people, a specific storm, and a specific act of defiance that's still reenacted every summer.
